Why it matters

Robotics is the rare extracurricular that keeps a record.

FIRST is judged, not just played. Students are recognized by name for leadership, for design, for innovation — and that recognition follows them out of the season.

  • 10 students worldwide earn the FIRST Leadership Award each FIRST Tech Challenge season. It was called the Dean’s List until recently, and it is the program’s highest honor for a student.
  • 200+ colleges, universities, companies and associations offer scholarships specifically to FIRST students — among them Rensselaer, Worcester Polytechnic, and Harvey Mudd.
  • $80M+ in scholarship value available to FIRST participants across the United States and Canada, through the FIRST Scholarship Program.

And the part that never shows up in a number: a student spends the season working beside practicing engineers, presenting to industry judges, and answering hard questions in front of strangers. The network starts before college does.

The path

Not a hypothesis. A route we have already walked.

One family’s decade in FIRST, from a middle-school LEGO team in Lakewood Ranch to a world championship and the program’s highest student honor. This is the arc we now mentor other students through.

  1. 2015

    The BotTanks begin

    A FIRST LEGO League team started in Lakewood Ranch and co-coached for the next nine seasons.

  2. 2020

    FIRST LEGO League Global Innovation Award

    Earned in a season the pandemic cut short. The work stood on its own anyway.

  3. 2024

    Florida State Finalist

    BotTanks reach the state championship — the ninth and final season of the team.

  4. 2025

    World Championship Finalist · 2nd Place, Design

    Java The Hutts compete on the world stage in FIRST Tech Challenge and place second for design — the award that judges how a team thinks, not just how it scores.

  5. 2025

    FIRST Leadership Award, Winner

    One of ten students named worldwide that season.

  6. 2026

    Leadership Award Finalist · Florida State Finalist

    A second Leadership Award honoree, and Ultronic reaches the state championship.

  7. 2028

    Designing the game itself

    Collaborating with FIRST game designers on the 2028 FIRST Tech Challenge season — from competing in the game to helping shape it.

What we do

Four ways in.

For students

Individual mentoring

For a student who wants more than a seat on a team. We work on the technical depth and the leadership record that the awards actually recognize — and place students onto teams where that work can count.

For teams

Season mentoring

Design reviews, engineering notebook coaching, judging preparation, competition strategy. We mentor teams we founded and teams we did not, including several now competing at the top of the state.

For schools & nonprofits

Standing up a program

We have started FIRST LEGO League teams from nothing and handed organizations a program they can run themselves — recruiting, coaching structure, equipment, season plan.

For events

Custom competitions

We design game challenges with complete rule books and run the events around them, for schools, districts, nonprofits and sponsors who want the format without the overhead.

What students actually learn

Real tools. Real deadlines. Real judges.

The competition is the framework, not the point. What students keep is the engineering habit underneath it.

  • CAD and mechanical designFusion 360, taught by certified mentors.
  • JavaThe language the FIRST Tech Challenge control system runs on.
  • AI-assisted developmentWorking fluently with the tools they will actually use.
  • Prototyping and fabricationPrinting, machining, building, breaking, rebuilding.
  • The engineering notebookDocumenting a decision well enough to defend it.
  • OutreachRunning events and teaching younger students. Judges notice; so does everyone else.
